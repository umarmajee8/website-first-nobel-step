Dear Applicant,

Thank you for showing interest in the internship program at First Noble Step Private Limited. We appreciate the time you took to share your application and resume with us, and we are pleased to see your interest in gaining practical professional experience.

We currently offer two internship options.

1. Physical Internship

Stipend: Rs. 12,500/Month

Our physical internship is available only for students based in Lahore. Selected interns work on-site with our team and gain practical exposure to real business tasks, professional communication, workplace culture, and project-based learning.

2. E-Internship

Our E-Internship is designed for students who are not based in Lahore or prefer a remote learning setup. This is a structured 6-week remote internship in which interns complete 6 practical projects related to real-world business and finance tasks. The program helps students build professional skills, improve their resume, and gain useful industry exposure from home.

Within the E-Internship program, we offer two categories:

A. Without Certificate

This option is completely free of cost and allows students to complete the assigned projects for learning and practical exposure.

B. With Certificate

This option includes a nominal fee covering certificate issuance, documentation, and delivery charges only. After successful completion of the internship requirements, the intern receives an official physical certificate on special company letterhead with an embossed company stamp, along with an Internship Completion Report.

Benefits: Practical project-based experience, stronger resume value, university internship proof support, improved interview confidence, portfolio-worthy work, and a Letter of Recommendation (subject to performance).

If you are based in Lahore, you may apply for the Physical Internship. If you are outside Lahore or prefer a remote option, we recommend the E-Internship Program.
